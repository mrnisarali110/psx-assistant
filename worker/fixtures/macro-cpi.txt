Inflation in Brief
1. CPI inflation General, increased by 10.3% on year-on-year basis in September 2026 as compared
to an increase of 11.1% in the previous month and 5.8% in September 2025. On month-on-month
basis, it increased by 1.3% in September 2026 as compared to an increase of 1.2% in the previous
month and an increase of 2.1% in September 2025.
2. CPI inflation Urban, increased by 10.1% on year-on-year basis in September 2026 as compared to
an increase of 10.4% in the previous month and an increase of 5.7% in September 2025. On month-
on-month basis, it increased by 1.3% in September 2026 as compared to an increase of 0.9% in the
previous month and an increase of 1.6% in September 2025.
3. CPI inflation Rural, increased by 10.5% on year-on-year basis in September 2026 as compared to
an increase of 12.2% in the previous month and an increase of 5.9% observed in September 2025.
